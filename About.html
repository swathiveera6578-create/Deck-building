COMMON QUESTIONS

About TimberNest

What type of outdoor projects do you create?

TimberNest focuses on outdoor living projects including decks, patios, pergolas, garden spaces, timber structures and outdoor entertainment areas.

Can projects be customised?

Yes. Designs can be planned around the available space, preferred materials, lifestyle and intended use of the outdoor area.

How does a project begin?

The process begins with a conversation about your outdoor space, requirements and ideas before moving into planning and design.

Do you help with material selection?

Yes. Material options can be discussed based on appearance, durability, maintenance and the overall design of the space.
